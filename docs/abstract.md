# Abstract

We compare two refinement mechanisms that arise in elliptic perimeter approximation and geometric acceleration. Ramanujan's second approximation to the ellipse perimeter acts along the eccentricity axis, while the geometric N-series acts along a resolution axis and cancels an even-power hierarchy in \(N^{-2}\). These mechanisms meet in the modulus variable of the Gauss-Legendre arithmetic-geometric mean iteration, where the normalized gap obeys the descending Landen contraction \(\delta_{n+1}\sim\tfrac14\delta_n^2\).

For every fixed integer \(m\ge2\), the paper proves

\[
\frac{\delta(q^m)}{\delta(q)^m}
=
4^{1-m}\bigl(1+4m q^2+O(q^4)\bigr),
\qquad q\to0.
\]

It also derives the linkage constant \(\alpha_n/\delta_n^2\to1/8\), consistent with the companion Gauss-Legendre coefficient law \(\alpha_{n+1}/\alpha_n^2\to1/2\). A guarded comparison with the Baker \(4{:}3\) trisection cascade separates Richardson-type resolution cancellation, modular gap contraction, and nonlinear cubic angular-error contraction.
